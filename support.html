技术支持
Support

输入与操作

输入 y=f(x)，也可省略 y=。支持四则运算、括号、右结合乘方、隐式乘法及 sin、cos、tan、sqrt、cbrt、abs、ln、log、exp。ln 为自然对数，log 为常用对数。角度单位在设置切换。

最多六条曲线。点击色点更换颜色；点击公式编辑；左滑删除，右滑置顶；函数菜单提供相同操作。删除后可在顶部撤销。拖动画布平移，双指缩放，长按读取所选函数坐标。

保存与恢复

“我的图组”可保存当前页面、新建空白页和恢复最近50条页面记录。收藏后修改会更新同一图组。工作区自动保存在本地；重启会恢复。清空历史不会删除已保存页面。

iPad 与不同窗口

宽窗口显示函数侧栏，窄窗口使用底部面板。可收起侧栏扩大画布。若切换窗口后键盘遮挡输入，请滚动函数面板或收起键盘；遇到异常可反馈窗口尺寸和系统版本。

Expressions and controls

Enter y=f(x), or omit y=. Supported operations include arithmetic, parentheses, right-associative powers, implicit multiplication, sin, cos, tan, sqrt, cbrt, abs, ln, log, and exp. ln means natural logarithm; log means base 10. Change radians or degrees in Settings.

Plot up to six curves. Tap a color dot or formula to edit it. Swipe left to delete or right to move a function to the top; the function menu also provides these actions. Undo a deletion using the top notification. Drag to pan, pinch to zoom, and hold to inspect a selected curve.

Pages, favorites, and history

My pages provides Save page, New page, and History for the 50 most recent pages. Favorited pages update as you edit them. The workspace restores after restart. Clearing history does not delete saved pages.

iPad and window sizes

Wide windows show a collapsible function sidebar. Narrow windows use a bottom panel. The layout adapts to rotation and split views while retaining your work.

隐私政策
Privacy policy

更新日期 / Updated: 2026-10-16

本地绘图与存储

Max 函数无需注册或登录。函数、参数、颜色、视图、收藏、图组和历史记录存储在设备本地。应用不向开发者上传这些内容，也不集成广告、追踪或第三方统计服务。我们不通过应用收集个人数据。

卸载应用可能删除本地数据；系统备份和设备恢复由你使用的平台及其设置管理。可在应用中删除图组或清空历史；删除图组不会自动清空当前画布。

导出与外部网页

只有你主动导出时，应用才通过系统分享面板将 PNG 交给你选择的目标。目标应用如何处理图片由其隐私政策约束。主动打开隐私或支持链接会使用浏览器；GitHub Pages 的托管请求和你主动提交的公开问题由 GitHub 处理。

联系与儿童使用

应用不设账号或用户间通信。可通过支持页面联系维护者。请不要在公开反馈中填写个人身份、收款或其他敏感信息。

Local plotting and storage

Max Functions does not require an account. Functions, parameters, colors, views, favorites, saved pages, and recent-page history stay on your device. The app does not upload them to the developer and includes no advertising, tracking, or analytics SDK. We do not collect personal data through the app.

Uninstalling may delete local data. Operating-system backups and device restoration follow your platform settings. You can delete saved pages or clear history in the app. Deleting a saved page does not automatically clear the current canvas.

Sharing and external websites

Only when you choose to export does the app send a PNG to your selected destination using the system share sheet. The receiving app's privacy policy applies. Opening support or privacy links launches a browser. GitHub handles requests to this GitHub Pages website and issues you choose to submit.

Support and children

The app has no accounts or user-to-user communication. Contact the maintainer through the support page. Do not include personal, payment, or other sensitive information in public issues.
